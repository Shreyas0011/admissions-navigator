# Admissions OS — Phase 2: Portals, Auto-Assignment & Seminar Day Mode

First, a finding from testing: I submitted the public application end-to-end in the running app and it **did work** — student `STU-2026-0042` was created and the portal opened. Two likely causes for what you saw: the email was already used (the form rejects duplicates), or a field failed validation silently because the form labels aren't wired to their inputs. I'll fix the label wiring and surface a clear top-of-form error either way.

## 1. Student sign-in

- New public route `/student-login`: email + password (the ones from the application form), redirects to `/portal`.
- Links both ways: "Already applied? Sign in" on the home page, "New applicant? Apply here" on the sign-in page.
- Staff sign-in stays sign-in only, as it is now.

## 2. Counsellor accounts (replaces "My Leads" in the admin sidebar)

- Admin sidebar item becomes **Counsellors**. Counsellors themselves still land on their own portal.
- Admin creates a counsellor with **email + password only**. Name/phone are filled in by the counsellor later.
- First login: a flag on the counsellor record forces a **reset password** screen (new password + confirm) before anything else loads. Flag clears on success.
- Counsellor portal gets a **My profile** section: name, phone, max active leads — the fields that belong to them in the counsellor record.
- Admin counsellor list → select a counsellor → see their allocated students, each student's admission progress, and every call log that counsellor recorded.

## 3. Automatic assignment (no more per-student "Auto assign")

- Policies gain a **published** state: many policies and pools may exist, exactly one policy can be published at a time (publishing one unpublishes the rest).
- When a student submits an application, the engine runs immediately against the published policy and its pool, and assigns a counsellor. No admin click.
- A "Run engine on backlog" button stays for students who arrived while no policy was published.
- Pools stay editable; newly created counsellors appear in the pool picker and are picked up by the next assignment.

## 4. Counsellor's students

- Counsellor selects a student and can: change their admission stage, and add a call entry with **date, time, what was discussed, outcome**.
- Saved calls are **immutable** — no edit, no delete, enforced in the database, not just the UI.
- Every stage change made by the counsellor is what the student sees in their portal timeline, and what the admin sees under that counsellor's student.

## 5. Seminar day mode

- Seminar builder gains a **ground-staff password** generated by the admin, shown/hidden on the seminar page so it can be handed over.
- Staff sign-in page gets a small **Seminar** toggle. It shows a card for any published seminar whose session date is today: name, time slot, venue, capacity.
- Ground staff enters **their name + the seminar password** — no account. They land on a small dashboard: seminar details, capacity, registered count, seats still free for walk-ins.

## 6. QR check-in

- Student portal: **View my QR** renders a static QR encoding the student's ID and name.
- Ground-staff dashboard: in-browser camera scanner.
  - Scan matches a registered booking → green **Mark attendance** button → recorded as present.
  - Scan has no booking → "Not registered", then checks free seats. If space exists, attendance can be marked as a **walk-in**; the dashboard counts update immediately. If full, entry is refused.
- Admin, for an active seminar: attendance view showing **registered vs present vs walk-ins** with student details, alongside the existing registration overview.

## Technical notes

- Database migration: `counsellors.must_reset_password`, `assignment_policies.is_published` (with a partial unique index), `event_sessions.ground_password`, `attendance.is_walk_in` + `walk_in_name`, `call_logs.called_at`, and RLS rules that deny update/delete on `call_logs`.
- Ground-staff access is password-scoped to a single session and handled server-side by a signed cookie, not a Supabase account.
- New domains follow the existing four layers: `routes` → `*.functions.ts` → `*.service.ts` → `*.repo.ts`, files kept under 200 lines.
- QR generation uses `qrcode.react`; scanning uses `html5-qrcode` as agreed earlier.
